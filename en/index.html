Le Manoir d'Aude is a fully renovated family home in the heart of the charming village of Peyriac-de-Mer, 200 metres from the local shops. It's divided into three independent apartments, Émeraude, Rubis and Péridot, each with its own character, and a shaded, fenced garden shared between the three, with a hammock, dining areas, sun loungers and a plancha area. Aude, Brigitte and Eric welcome you as their own guests, whichever apartment you choose.

What to do in Peyriac-de-Mer

The village sits at the heart of the Narbonnaise Regional Nature Park, surrounded by the lagoons of the Corbières Maritimes. Here's what's on your doorstep.

Nature on your doorstep

  • Étang du Doul: a salt lake almost as saline as the Dead Sea. The trail around it climbs to the Mouré hill for a 360° view over the lagoons.
  • The Old Saltworks: a 2 km boardwalk trail through the former saltworks, a protected nature reserve where you'll spot flamingos, black-winged stilts and egrets.

Nearby excursions

  • Fontfroide Abbey (6 km): a medieval Cistercian abbey with magnificent gardens.
  • Sigean African Reserve (7 km): a large African wildlife park, great for families.
  • Narbonne: a medieval city with Roman remains, a cathedral, and the Les Halles market.
  • Gruissan: a colourful fishing village on its own lagoon, between salt lakes and sandy beaches.

On the water

  • Rent a kayak or paddleboard to explore the lagoons and islands at your own pace.
  • Windsurfing and kitesurfing lessons are available from local schools along the coast.

Food & wine

  • The village's own restaurants serve homemade, seasonal cooking.
  • Local winegrowers are happy to open their cellars for a tasting, with wines that carry notes of garrigue and red berries, right in the heart of the Corbières.
