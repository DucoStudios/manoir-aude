Le Manoir d'Aude is a fully renovated family home in the heart of the charming village of Peyriac-de-Mer. The Émeraude apartment blends old and modern on the first floor, perfect for relaxing, enjoying the lagoon scenery, and discovering the Corbières Maritimes at your own pace. Aude, Brigitte and Eric welcome you as their own guests.
