The spirit of the house

A family home in Languedoc

Le Manoir d'Aude is a 19th-century townhouse, in the heart of a village surrounded by the lagoons of the Corbière Maritime, within the Narbonnaise Regional Nature Park.

Guests come as couples, families or friends to enjoy a large shaded garden shared between the three apartments, steps from the salt pans and the lagoon where flamingos gather.

Shared garden

A large shaded garden, shared by all three apartments, with an outdoor sitting area for long summer evenings.

Location

200 metres from the village shops, a few minutes' walk from the lagoons of Peyriac-de-Mer.

Total independence

Three separate apartments, each with its own entrance, for a stay in complete freedom.

Where to sleep

Three apartments, one family home

Each with its own character, all steps from the shared garden and the village.

Worth discovering

The Narbonnaise Nature Park

The village sits at the heart of the Narbonnaise Regional Nature Park, surrounded by the lagoons of the Corbière Maritime. Here's what's waiting just outside the door.

  • Étang du Doul & the old salt pans
  • Fontfroide Abbey
  • Sigean African Reserve
  • Narbonne & its covered market
  • Gruissan & the beaches
  • Kayaking, paddleboarding, windsurfing, kitesurfing
